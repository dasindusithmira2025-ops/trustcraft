You are designing the competition-grade UI/UX for “TrustCraft”, an AI-assisted trust platform for home services in Sri Lanka.

THIS IS NOT A GENERIC HOME-SERVICES MARKETPLACE.

Do not design another “Uber for plumbers”, handyman directory, ecommerce marketplace, dashboard full of cards, or category-grid application.

The goal is to create an exceptionally polished, creative, memorable mobile product concept suitable for a national UI/UX innovation competition. The judges will care heavily about visual design, usability, UX creativity, accessibility, originality, interaction design, and how intelligently the interface solves the real user problem.

The final result should feel like a real premium startup product designed by an elite product design team.

========================================================
PRODUCT THESIS
========================================================

TrustCraft solves a problem of INFORMAL TRUST.

Customers often do not know:
- what type of technician they actually need
- who they can trust
- whether someone is genuinely qualified
- why one technician is a better match than another
- what the job should reasonably involve
- what they are agreeing to pay
- whether the completed work has trustworthy evidence

At the same time, genuinely skilled independent professionals struggle to prove their reliability outside personal referrals and social networks.

TrustCraft turns invisible trust into visible evidence.

Core product philosophy:

UNDERSTAND → MATCH → AGREE → RESOLVE → TRUST

TrustCraft allows customers to SHOW or DESCRIBE the actual problem instead of requiring them to understand the correct service category.

The system then:
1. intelligently structures the problem
2. asks useful clarification questions
3. identifies an appropriate service category
4. surfaces a small set of relevant verified professionals
5. explains WHY each professional is recommended
6. provides an evidence-backed Trust Score
7. makes inspection fees and quotations transparent
8. creates explicit customer approval before work
9. tracks the job lifecycle
10. creates verified service history and reviews after completion

AI assists decision-making.
AI must NEVER pretend to replace a qualified professional.

========================================================

Make this progressive transformation a core visual characteristic of TrustCraft.
